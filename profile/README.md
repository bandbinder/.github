BandBinder keeps your charts, setlists and rehearsal notes in one place - solo or whole bands. It's the same app on all platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS. The app is free on every device you own, forever: no ads, no song limit, custom charts, fully offline Performance Mode, import and export anytime. Sharing with your band and syncing across devices is the only part that costs anything. Early adopters get FREE sync with a coupon code.
